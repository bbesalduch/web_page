Relax & Restore

Wellness

A Sanctuary for the Senses

At Hotel Can Quetglas, wellness is woven into every corner. From the gentle embrace of our saltwater pool to the shade of century-old trees in our Mediterranean garden, every space invites you to slow down, breathe deeply, and reconnect with yourself.

Natural Oasis

Saltwater Pool

Immerse yourself in our saltwater pool, gentler on your skin and more natural than chlorine. The mineral-rich water leaves your skin soft and refreshed, while the serene atmosphere invites you to unwind.

Relax on our sun loungers, enjoy a refreshing drink from the pool bar, and let the Mallorcan sun work its magic.

Century-Old Beauty

Mediterranean Garden

Our garden is a living legacy, home to century-old trees that have witnessed generations of guests seeking peace and tranquility. Orange and lemon trees, palm trees, and native Mediterranean plants create a fragrant sanctuary.

Find your perfect spot under the shade, read a book, or simply enjoy the birdsong and the gentle rustle of leaves.

Massage & Treatments

by THINKCOSMETIC

Indulge in our selection of massages and treatments using Alimenta Spa Mediterráneo products, 100% organic and crafted in Mallorca with natural ingredients from the Mediterranean.

Relaxing Massage

Full body massage with organic sweet almond oil to relieve stress and promote deep relaxation.

Decontracting Massage

Therapeutic massage with mint and rosemary oil to release muscle tension and revitalize the body.

Cupping Treatment

Ancient therapy combining suction and massage to improve circulation and promote natural healing.

Ayurvedic Massage

Traditional Indian-inspired massage with warm almond oil to balance body, mind, and vital energy.

Treatments available in our massage room, on the rooftop terrace, or in the privacy of your room.
