Enduring Legacy

A 1908 modernist palace designed by a disciple of Gaudí, standing as the cultural pulse of El Terreno, surrounded by century-old trees, local art galleries, and the bohemian spirit of 1960s Palma.

Our spaces feature iconic design pieces and 9 rooms embodying a minimalist aesthetic enhanced with artisanal Mallorcan touches.

Wellness

Saltwater Pool & Garden

Immerse yourself in our saltwater pool, gentler on your skin and more natural than chlorine. Surrounded by our Mediterranean garden with century-old trees, it's the perfect oasis to escape the heat.

Relax on our sun loungers, enjoy a refreshing drink from the pool bar, and let the Mallorcan sun work its magic. Our garden provides shaded spots for those who prefer to read or simply enjoy the tranquility.
