Lasting Heritage

A 1908 modernist palace designed by a disciple of Gaudí, standing as the cultural pulse of El Terreno, surrounded by century-old trees, the pine forest of Bellver Castle, and the bohemian spirit of 1960s Palma.

Our spaces feature iconic design pieces and 9 rooms embodying a minimalist aesthetic enhanced with artisanal Mallorcan touches.
